About Relucent

Relucent is an advanced photo editing application for Android. It focuses on powerful editing tools, a streamlined workflow, and keeping your photos under your control.

Photo editing and AI-assisted features are processed locally on your device. Relucent does not require an account and does not upload your photos to external servers.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Relucent is developed and operated by an independent individual developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information Relucent accesses, stores, and processes when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Relucent does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, location, device identifiers, or advertising identifiers.

Relucent does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Photos and Media

Relucent accesses photos on your device when you choose to import them for editing. Photo editing and image processing are performed locally on your device.

Relucent does not upload your photos, videos, or editing projects to external servers or cloud storage.

Photos and editing projects may be stored locally on your device to provide editing and project functionality. Exported photos are saved to your device's photo gallery when you choose to export them.

AI and Image Processing

Relucent uses on-device image processing and machine learning technologies for features such as subject and object segmentation. These processes run locally on your device. Your images are not sent to remote AI services.

Permissions

Relucent may request access to photos and media on your device when required for importing or exporting images. These permissions are used solely to provide the application's photo editing functionality.

Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Relucent does not currently use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, advertising identifiers, or third-party crash reporting services.

Third-Party Services

Relucent does not currently send user data to external services or use third-party services to collect personal information.

Android Backup

Relucent allows Android's standard backup functionality. Depending on your device and backup settings, locally stored app data such as editing preferences, edit projects, and imported image copies may be included in your device's backup. Temporary cache files are not included in standard app backup.

Photo Metadata

When exporting JPEG images, Relucent can preserve standard photo metadata if you enable the "Keep photo metadata" option.

GPS and other location metadata are excluded by default. Location metadata is only included when exporting a JPEG if you explicitly enable both metadata preservation and the location preservation option.

PNG and WebP exports do not preserve the original photo's EXIF metadata.

Data Storage and Deletion

Relucent may store editing projects, preferences, temporary files, and imported image copies locally on your device. These files remain on your device until they are deleted through the application, the device's storage settings, or by uninstalling the application, where applicable.

Exported photos saved to your device's gallery remain under your control and can be deleted using your device's normal photo management tools.

Data Sharing

Relucent does not sell or share your personal information. When you explicitly use Android's sharing functionality, the selected image may be passed to another application installed on your device through Android's standard sharing system.

Children's Privacy

Relucent is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
